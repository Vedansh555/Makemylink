# Market Size and Revenue Signals: Link-in-Bio and Creator Storefronts (as of Oct 2026)

Source reliability key, used throughout: **[Primary]** means a company statement or a press report quoting one. **[Analyst]** means an estimate from Sacra or Contrary: researched, but not disclosed by the company. **[Aggregator]** means CB Insights, Clay, vendor blogs or market-report mills: low reliability. All private-company revenue figures are estimates unless marked otherwise.

## Linktree: users, paying users, revenue, funding, valuation, layoffs, acquisitions, strategy

### Takeaway
Linktree is the category leader with about 70M registered users. Revenue estimates are only about $37M (2023) to $55.5M (2024). That works out to under $1 of revenue per registered user per year, which points to a very low paid share (likely around 1–3%). Its strategy is buy-and-shut-down consolidation (Bento, Koji, Plann, Fingertip) plus a push into commerce and social tools. It raised about $166M at a $1.3B valuation in 2022, and there have been repeated layoffs since.

### Cited Findings
- Users: 70M+ users as of April 2025 [Analyst] — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/linktree/). "Around 70 million" as of Nov 2025 [Primary press] — [SmartCompany, 17 Nov 2025](https://www.smartcompany.com.au/startupsmart/linktree-acquires-leibovich-brothers-backed-fingertip-to-shut-it-down/). Earlier data points: 50M+ users in May 2024 (Zaccaria) — [TechCrunch, May 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/05/22/linktree-surpasses-50m-users-rolls-out-beta-social-commerce-program/?rand=23331); 24M users at the 2022 raise — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2284606); 31M users and $33M ARR (2022), growing 29% y/y [Analyst] — [Sacra Beacons page](https://sacra.com/c/beacons).
- Paying users: never disclosed. Linktree declined to say what share of its 24M users paid, and paid tiers then ran from free to $21/mo — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2284606).
- Revenue: $37M in 2023 and $55.5M in 2024, about 50% growth [Analyst] — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/linktree/). An older Sacra figure of $49.09M for 2023 conflicts with this — [Sacra valuation page](https://sacra.com/c/linktree/valuation/). A Huxiu (China) piece estimates ARR at roughly $100M (2025) and calls a $200M claim exaggerated — [Huxiu](https://m.huxiu.com/aibrief/11160.html). Template-seller sites claim about $120M ARR for 2025, unsourced [Aggregator, low reliability] — [businessmodelcanvastemplate](https://businessmodelcanvastemplate.com/products/linktree-swot-analysis).
- Profitability claim: Linktree says it has been profitable since its 2016 launch and was bootstrapped until 2020 — [SmartCompany, Nov 2025](https://www.smartcompany.com.au/startupsmart/linktree-acquires-leibovich-brothers-backed-fingertip-to-shut-it-down/).
- Funding: $15M Series A (2020), $59M Series B (Mar 2021), $152M Series C (Mar 2022) — [SmartCompany](https://www.smartcompany.com.au/startupsmart/linktree-acquires-leibovich-brothers-backed-fingertip-to-shut-it-down/). Sacra gives $165.7M total and a $1.3B valuation (2022), led by Coatue and Index — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/linktree/).
- Pricing (current per Sacra): Starter $8, Pro $15, Premium $35 per month ($6/$12/$30 annual). Seller fee on digital products is 9% on Starter/Pro and 0% on Premium — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/linktree/). Contrary cites Linktree seller fees of 12%/9%/0% by plan, with 12% presumably on the free plan — [Contrary](https://research.contrary.com/company/stan).
- Acquisitions (5): Odesli (2021); Bento (early 2023, Sequoia-backed); Koji (late 2023, shut down about a month later; Koji had raised $36M per Sacra); Plann (Sydney social scheduler, about Aug 2024, inferred from "15 months before" Nov 2025); Fingertip (Nov 2025, users migrated, product shut at end of April 2026). All terms undisclosed — [SmartCompany](https://www.smartcompany.com.au/startupsmart/linktree-acquires-leibovich-brothers-backed-fingertip-to-shut-it-down/); [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/linktree/).
- Layoffs: 17% (about 50 roles) in 2022 — [SmartCompany](https://www.smartcompany.com.au/information-technology/linktree-sacks-employees-tech/). 27% in 2023, concentrated in ANZ, shifting hiring to the US, which became the largest market (announced alongside Bento) — [SmartCompany](https://www.smartcompany.com.au/startupsmart/news/linktree-lays-off-27-workers-hitting-anz-staff-hardest-favour-us-hires/); [B&T](https://www.bandt.com.au/linktree-slashes-27-of-global-headcount-australia-new-zealand-hit-hardest/). A Blind tracker lists Australian layoffs on Aug 18, Sep 13 and Oct 31, 2025 (30/50/70 people) [Aggregator, low reliability, unverified] — [Blind](https://www.teamblind.com/layoffs/company/linktree). No 2026 layoffs found.
- Strategy shifts: beta social-commerce/affiliate program in 2024 — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/05/22/linktree-surpasses-50m-users-rolls-out-beta-social-commerce-program/?rand=23331). Sacra frames it as "Link in bio to Creator OS" and notes a new performance/affiliate revenue stream — [Sacra chat](https://sacra.com/chat/h/adb493e6-a1eb-4e5e-a6f8-a07acc8957d3/); [Sacra chat](https://sacra.com/chat/h/ad43903e-6e4a-4061-acd1-bbd7c7593f20/). The Plann deal adds social scheduling, competing with Later.

### Inferences
- About $55M of revenue on about 70M users is roughly $0.80 per user per year. At about $10/mo ARPU (~$120/yr), that implies roughly 450k paying subscribers, about 0.6–1% of registered users. This is my rough estimate and not disclosed; many registered accounts are inactive.
- Revenue growth (~50%) outpacing user growth suggests monetization is now driven by price increases and commerce fees, not signups.
- Linktree buys competitors and shuts them down. For a new entrant, a plausible exit is an acqui-hire or user-base sale to Linktree, which has done five such deals.

### Gaps
- No disclosed 2025 or 2026 revenue, ARR or paid-subscriber count. No acquisition prices. No confirmed 2026 layoffs or new funding.

## Stan Store: revenue/ARR, creators, GMV, growth, pricing, funding

### Takeaway
Stan is the strongest evidence that the creator-storefront model (paid-only, $29/mo, 0% take rate) monetizes far better per user than freemium link-in-bio. It reached about $28M ARR by end-2024 with only about $5M raised and about 30 staff, and it is profitable. Growth slowed sharply after 2024 (+24% in 2025 per Sacra), and churn is high (about 13%/month).

### Cited Findings
- ARR: $1.7M (start of 2023), then $14.7M (end of 2023, 8.6x), then $28.3M (end of 2024); $30M disclosed in May 2025 (Stan's last disclosure); about $35M at end-2025 and about $40M in Apr 2026 [Analyst, unverified] — [Contrary](https://research.contrary.com/company/stan); [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/stan). Sacra says growth slowed from about 93% (2024) to 24% (2025) — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/stans-shopify-vs-whops-amazon/).
- 2024 revenue about $25M (Forbes), 40% EBITDA margin; about 30 employees, about $1M revenue per employee — [Contrary](https://research.contrary.com/company/stan).
- Creators: 80,000+ active (Sacra, 2025); 85K active as of Aug 2026; 59K+ creators earned money in 2025 — [Contrary](https://research.contrary.com/company/stan); [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/stan).
- GMV (creator sales): $300M cumulative over three years (Sept 2025), $400M+ (Jan 2026), $600M+ (Aug 2026), so roughly $340M/yr run rate now. 2025 sales: 2.2M digital downloads, 300K courses, 236K memberships, $67 average order. About 45% of GMV comes from TikTok/IG [unverified] — [Contrary](https://research.contrary.com/company/stan).
- Churn: about 13% of customers per month (2024) — [Contrary](https://research.contrary.com/company/stan).
- Pricing: Creator $29/mo ($300/yr), Creator Pro $99/mo ($948/yr), 0% Stan fee (Stripe 2.9% + $0.30), 14-day trial, no free plan. "Stanley" AI add-on: $45/mo on Instagram; the LinkedIn version passed $1M ARR by Jul 2026 — [Contrary](https://research.contrary.com/company/stan).
- Funding: $5M seed in Jan 2022 led by Forerunner at a $25M valuation (plus Pear VC). Undisclosed investments from Steven Bartlett (May 2025) and Gary Vaynerchuk (Sept 2025). Profitable since the seed — [Contrary](https://research.contrary.com/company/stan).
- Growth story: founded Jan 2020, launched May 2021. Growth was driven by creator-affiliate/"Stan Creator" referral loops and "make money online" creator content — [Sacra chat](https://sacra.com/chat/h/7ca39735-0ffb-42a2-919b-80bca1932b5a); [Contrary](https://research.contrary.com/report/stan).
- Competitor context: Whop is at about $142M annualized revenue (Oct 2025, +255% y/y) [Analyst] — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/whop-at-142m-revenue). Whop reports $2.67B lifetime GMV, about 184k sellers and 14.2M users (Feb 2026), plus a $200M Tether-led round at a $1.6B valuation [mixed Primary/Aggregator] — [RockWater](https://chris-erwin-rockwater-newsletter.beehiiv.com/p/tether-invests-200m-in-whop-clipping-grey-markets-and-the-1-6b-creator-marketplace); [Sacra Whop](https://sacra.com/research/whop).

### Inferences
- $28–35M ARR on about 80K creators is about $30–36/mo ARPU, roughly 3–4x Linktree's per-paying-user ARPU and about 400x its per-registered-user revenue. Charging everyone, with no free tier, and selling "make money" outcomes is what drives this.
- 13% monthly churn means Stan must replace most of its base each year. Its slowdown suggests the "side-hustle creator" segment is saturating. Whop is taking the high-growth marketplace and take-rate position.

### Gaps
- No official 2025/2026 ARR from Stan. No verified paid-customer count separate from "active creators."

## Beacons: users, funding, revenue

### Takeaway
Beacons, backed by a16z and Y Combinator, raised about $29M ($6M seed in 2021 plus a $23M Series A in Nov 2023). It has never disclosed revenue, and the only public revenue data are early Sacra estimates (about $2M in 2021). Early unit economics were weak: about 0.5% freemium conversion and 16–18% monthly gross revenue churn.

### Cited Findings
- $600K angel round, then a $6M seed in May 2021 led by a16z (Atelier, Mantis/Chainsmokers, Night, LOUD, Crush). At that time it had 250,000+ creator accounts and 3,000+ new per day — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/05/24/beacons-seed-round-creator-profiles/amp); [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20210524005214/en).
- $23M Series A in Nov 2023 [Aggregator] — [Clay](https://www.clay.com/dossier/beacons-funding); [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/beacons/financials). Sacra lists total Beacons funding as $29M — [Sacra Linktree](https://sacra.com/c/linktree/). Aggregators conflict on totals ($23M up to $52M).
- Revenue: Sacra puts 2021 revenue at $1.94M and gives an "ARR ~$3.7M, Jan 2021" figure that looks internally inconsistent. Other Sacra metrics: 0.5% freemium conversion rate, 16–18% monthly gross revenue churn (versus ConvertKit's 5%), LTV/CAC of 4–11x, and 9% fee on the free plan — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/beacons); [Sacra article](https://sacra.com/p/the-creator-storefront-growing-209).
- Pricing (per Sacra): paid plans $10–$50/mo, and the free plan takes 9% of sales — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/beacons).

### Inferences
- A 0.5% freemium conversion rate is a key benchmark. It suggests that free link-in-bio pages convert far below generic SaaS freemium (2–8%).

### Gaps
- No current user counts, revenue or valuation found. The "millions of users" claim comes only from review sites.

## Others: Later (Linkin.bio), Komi, Snipfeed, Hoo.be, Taplink, Lnk.bio, Bio.link, Liinks, Carrd, indie products

### Takeaway
Most second-tier players raised small seed rounds ($5–12M) and disclose no revenue. The best public revenue signals come from bootstrapped founders. Liinks reached about $20–25K MRR solo, and Carrd (adjacent one-page builder) reached $1–2M+ ARR with two people.

### Cited Findings
- Later / Linkin.bio: acquired by Mavrck in Apr 2022 (backed by a $135M Summit Partners investment, price undisclosed). Later had raised less than $2M. Linkin.bio had generated 2B+ pageviews since 2016, and Later had about 7M users. In 2024 the combined company rebranded as Later, with 8M+ users and a "Later Link in Bio" product — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/04/27/mavrck-raises-135m-buys-linkin-bio-maker-later-in-creator-and-influencer-marketing-consolidation); [BetaKit](https://betakit.com/later-acquired-by-us-influencer-marketing-platform-mavrck/); [BIV](https://biv.com/article/2022/04/vancouvers-later-acquired-after-new-owner-raises-us135m). Revenue was not disclosed.
- Komi: $5M seed in Dec 2022 led by Contour, $10/mo subscription — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/12/08/komi-a-landing-page-tool-for-content-creators-raises-5m-seed-round). A $12M Series A in Jul 2023 led by RTP comes from a single database entry [Aggregator, unverified] — [rss.boorghani](https://rss.boorghani.com/?p=135984).
- Snipfeed: $5.5M seed in Aug 2021 — [Built In LA](https://www.builtinla.com/articles/snipfeed-raises-5m-seed-round-link-in-bio-influencer-content). CB Insights shows $10.59M over 5 rounds and an Apr 2024 "acquisition" entry [Aggregator, unverified] — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/snipfeed/financials).
- Liinks (solo founder Charlie Clark, unfunded side project): $1K MRR when he posted on Indie Hackers, $20K MRR with 4,200 subscribers (about $4.76 ARPU) by mid-2023, and a $25K MRR headline. Reported as $25K MRR about 3 years after official launch (another source says about 4), with server costs around $10/day. Growth came mostly from Instagram, an "auto-add links from IG posts" feature, Twitter build-in-public and low pricing versus Linktree — [Superframeworks, Aug 2023](https://superframeworks.com/blog/liinks); [Indie Hustle](https://indiehustle.beehiiv.com/p/simple-linkinbio-tool-making-20000-mrr); [Liinks press](https://blog.liinks.co/press).
- Carrd (AJ, adjacent one-page builder often used as link-in-bio): bootstrapped to about $30K MRR (2020), then raised $2M for expertise rather than need. Later reached $100K+ MRR, about $2M ARR (2023, per an IH comment) and 4M+ sites with two people — [SaaS Club](https://saasclub.io/?p=6013581); [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/growing-carrd-to-2m-arr-in-a-crowded-market-with-zero-marketing-by-limiting-features-dbeoP4bDlEL3zzsivdgj); [Fathom](https://usefathom.com/podcast/carrd).
- Other funded players (per Sacra): Koji $36M (acquired by Linktree), Stir $20M, Linkfire $2.7M; 40+ competitors; link-in-bio fees of $5–24/mo versus $29–299 for vertical SaaS — [Sacra Beacons](https://sacra.com/c/beacons).
- soc.bio and ForgeBio show many new indie link-in-bio launches on Indie Hackers ("I know there are a million of them"), but none share revenue — [Indie Hackers tcat](https://www.indiehackers.com/tcat).

### Inferences
- The realistic bootstrapped ceiling in plain link-in-bio is about $20–25K MRR after 3+ years, built on a cheap price and one distribution wedge such as an Instagram-specific feature. Going beyond that (Carrd) took a broader product such as a one-page site builder.

### Gaps
- No public numbers found for Hoo.be, Taplink, Lnk.bio, Bio.link or a recent Liinks figure (post-2023). Searches returned nothing reliable. No Later Link in Bio revenue split.

## Overall creator economy and link-in-bio market size; creators earning money; freemium conversion

### Takeaway
The creator economy is large ($250B, heading to about $480B by 2027 per Goldman), but almost all of that is brand deals and ad payouts, not tool spend. Link-in-bio tool market estimates are low-quality and range from $0.4B to $1.8B for 2025. A bottom-up estimate gives the dedicated link-in-bio/storefront software market as roughly $0.2–0.5B in subscription revenue today (Linktree about $55M+, Stan about $35M, Whop much larger but a marketplace, and long-tail players). Only about 4% of creators earn more than $100K per year.

### Cited Findings
- Goldman Sachs: creator economy TAM goes from about $250B (2023) to $480B by 2027. About 50M global creators growing 10–20% per year; brand deals make up about 70% of creator income; about 4% of creators earn more than $100K per year — [Goldman Sachs](https://www.goldmansachs.com/insights/pages/the-creator-economy-could-approach-half-a-trillion-dollars-by-2027.html); [Kolaking summary](https://kolaking.substack.com/p/the-creator-economy-could-approach). A later Goldman estimate gives 67M creators in 2025 and 107M by 2030 — [Contrary](https://research.contrary.com/company/stan); [blog.mean.ceo](https://blog.mean.ceo/?p=5743).
- Citi gives 120M+ creators and about $60B in revenue — [NAB Amplify](https://amplify.nabshow.com/?p=149314). MBO Partners found 9% of independent creators made more than $100K in 2024 — [blog.mean.ceo](https://blog.mean.ceo/?p=5743).
- Link-in-bio market reports [Aggregator, low reliability, inconsistent scope]: Market Intelo puts 2025 at $0.4B, reaching $0.95B by 2034 (12.3% CAGR) — [Market Intelo](https://marketintelo.com/report/link-in-bio-tools-market). StrategyMRC gives $1.2B (2025) reaching $3.2B by 2032, and Dataintelo $1.8B (2025) reaching $6.4B by 2034, both cited by vendor blogs — [linkie.bio](https://linkie.bio/blog/link-in-bio-tools); [mewayz](https://mewayz.com/te/blog/40-link-in-bio-and-creator-tool-statistics-for-2026-market-size-usage-trends).
- Freemium conversion benchmarks: OpenView 2022 (secondhand) gives a 2–5% median and 5–10% for top performers; Lenny Rachitsky (via Userpilot) gives 6–8% for self-serve; First Page Sage gives 3.7%; a 2026 ChartMogul/Growth Unhinged/ProductLed survey of 200 products found an 8% median — [Single Grain](https://www.singlegrain.com/blog/ms/free-to-paid-conversion-rates/); [Userpilot](https://userpilot.com/blog/freemium-to-premium). Link-in-bio specific: Beacons at about 0.5% — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/beacons).

### Inferences
- Creator-tool conversion runs far below SaaS benchmarks. Beacons converted about 0.5% and Linktree an implied ~1%, while Stan converts 100% because it has no free plan. A new freemium entrant should plan on 0.5–2% free-to-paid.
- Spending power is concentrated: the roughly 4% of creators earning more than $100K, plus monetizing "side-hustle" sellers like Stan's 59K earning creators, are the buyers. Most of the 50–67M "creators" will never pay.
- Realistic outcomes for a new entrant:
  - Bootstrapped generic link-in-bio: $5–25K MRR over 2–4 years, for example around 1,000–4,000 subscribers at $5–8.
  - Niche storefront priced at about $20–30/mo: needs about 500–2,000 paying creators for $10–50K MRR.
  - Venture scale ($10M+ ARR): has historically required either a viral creator-affiliate loop (Stan) or a commerce take-rate (Whop).

### Gaps
- No credible primary market-sizing report for link-in-bio. No source on the total number of paying link-in-bio subscribers industry-wide.

## Bootstrapped success stories: how small products reached $10K–$100K+ MRR and how fast

### Takeaway
Documented cases are few. Liinks reached about $20–25K MRR solo in about 3 years. Carrd went from $0 to about $30K MRR before raising, and later passed $100K MRR with two people. Stan is "nearly bootstrapped": a $5M seed took it from $1.7M to $14.7M ARR in 2023. The common traits are low price or a narrow wedge, distribution native to one platform, and very low costs.

### Cited Findings
- Liinks: $1K, then $20K MRR (4,200 subscribers), then about $25K MRR in about 3 years. Solo founder, around $10/day in server costs, growth from Instagram plus IH/Twitter build-in-public — [Superframeworks](https://superframeworks.com/blog/liinks); [Indie Hustle](https://indiehustle.beehiiv.com/p/simple-linkinbio-tool-making-20000-mrr).
- Carrd: zero-marketing growth by limiting features; about $30K MRR (2020), $1M+ ARR, then $100K+ MRR with 2 people — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/growing-carrd-to-2m-arr-in-a-crowded-market-with-zero-marketing-by-limiting-features-dbeoP4bDlEL3zzsivdgj); [SaaS Club](https://saasclub.io/?p=6013581).
- Linktree itself was bootstrapped (and profitable) from 2016 to 2020 before raising its Series A — [SmartCompany](https://www.smartcompany.com.au/startupsmart/linktree-acquires-leibovich-brothers-backed-fingertip-to-shut-it-down/).
- Stan: about $0 to $1.7M ARR (2021–22), then $14.7M (2023), then $28.3M (2024), with $5M raised and profitable — [Contrary](https://research.contrary.com/company/stan).

### Inferences
- Reaching $10K MRR in a crowded generic category typically takes 1–3 years. Faster paths depend on a distribution hook, such as Stan's creator-affiliate referral program or Liinks' Instagram post auto-linking.

### Gaps
- No verified Open Startup or X MRR figures found for Bio.link, Lnk.bio, Taplink, Hoo.be or newer 2025–2026 indie link-in-bio tools. A targeted X/IH search could fill this.
