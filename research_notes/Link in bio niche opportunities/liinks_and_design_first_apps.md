# Liinks and Design-First Link-in-Bio Competitors: Users, Growth Playbooks, Traction

Research date: 2026-10-26. Dates are given on every figure. Many traction numbers come from 2021-2024 sources and are flagged as dated.

## 1. Liinks company profile: founders, launch, country, funding, users, paid share, pricing, features

### Takeaway
Liinks (liinks.co) is a bootstrapped, one-person US company run by Charlie Clark (Brooklyn, NY). It started in 2020-21, takes no outside funding, and was at about $25K MRR with about 5,300 paying customers in October 2024. It has no free plan, and paid tiers currently start at $5/mo. The "10,000+ users, about 50% paid" claim seems to come from putting two numbers side by side: the homepage's "10,000+ creators & businesses" (current) and Starter Story's roughly 5,300 paying customers (Oct 2024). No source states a 50% paid share directly.

### Cited Findings
**Founder, location, funding, team**
- Founder: Charlie Clark, solo, with a background in design and software engineering. Based in Brooklyn, NY, USA. No employees. He had recently quit his full-time job to work on Liinks full time (as of Oct 2024). — [Starter Story, published 2024-10-22](https://starterstory.com/stories/liinks-supercharge-your-link-in-bio)
- The About page says Liinks began in 2020 as a side project and is "independently built and operated" with "no investors to please." — [Liinks About](https://www.liinks.co/about)
- Funded from savings. The LLC was incorporated via Stripe Atlas for $500. — [Starter Story](https://starterstory.com/stories/liinks-supercharge-your-link-in-bio)
- The MVP took about 2 weeks because Clark reused code and UX from his earlier app, VSUAL. Stack: React, Node.js, MongoDB, a DigitalOcean VPS and AWS S3 (about $50/mo). — [Starter Story](https://starterstory.com/stories/liinks-supercharge-your-link-in-bio)

**Launch date (sources conflict)**
- Starter Story lists the start date as February 2020. — [Starter Story](https://starterstory.com/stories/liinks-supercharge-your-link-in-bio)
- Indie Hustle, citing the Product Hunt post, puts the soft launch at the end of January 2021. — [Indie Hustle, 2023-12-17](https://indiehustle.beehiiv.com/p/simple-linkinbio-tool-making-20000-mrr)
- Clark's own Indie Hackers post says $25K MRR came about 4.5 years after launch, while podcast and press blurbs say "3 years after it officially launched." — [Indie Hackers post (search snippet; page returned 403)](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/rRxyYFmG95zcSKSYdtM8); [Liinks press page](https://blog.liinks.co/press)

**Revenue and customer timeline (dated)**
- First customers came about 2 months after launch, paying $3. He reached about $1,000 MRR roughly 4 months later after adding features, a 14-day free trial and paid Instagram marketing. Two months after that, he raised the price from $3 to $3.99 and hit $2,000 MRR. MRR was about $15,000 in Dec 2022 and $20,000 in Jul 2023. — [Indie Hustle, 2023-12-17](https://indiehustle.beehiiv.com/p/simple-linkinbio-tool-making-20000-mrr)
- At $20K MRR there were 4,200 subscribers. — [Superframeworks](https://superframeworks.com/blog/liinks)
- In Oct 2024: about $25K/mo in the article body (the page header shows $31.5K/mo, a conflict that may reflect a later update), about $250K revenue over the trailing 12 months, and about 5,300 paying customers (about 4,800 Premium and 500 Pro). Margins were 80-90% depending on ad spend, and growth over the last 12 months was 11%. Growth had slowed because of lower ad spend and the founder's time after a baby arrived in Oct 2023. His goal was $30K MRR by Sep 2025. — [Starter Story](https://starterstory.com/stories/liinks-supercharge-your-link-in-bio)

**User count and paid-share claim**
- The current homepage says "Loved by 10,000+ creators & businesses." — [liinks.co](https://liinks.co)
- About 5,300 paying customers as of Oct 2024. — [Starter Story](https://starterstory.com/stories/liinks-supercharge-your-link-in-bio)
- The free tier was removed because of spam and phishing abuse. The founder says this increased the number of paying users. — [Starter Story](https://starterstory.com/stories/liinks-supercharge-your-link-in-bio)

**Pricing (current, Oct 2026)**
- Starter: $5/mo or $48/yr. Includes unlimited blocks and socials, a liinks.co URL, advanced styling and themes, analytics and integrations, multi-tab navigation, and QR codes. — [liinks.co](https://liinks.co)
- Pro: $12/mo or $120/yr per pack of 5 profiles. Adds multi-profile management, custom domains, tab-linking, custom social share previews, and beta features such as AI Chat. — [liinks.co](https://liinks.co)
- No free plan. Users can build the whole page for free before going live, and every plan starts with a 14-day free trial. — [liinks.co](https://liinks.co)
- Historical pricing: Premium launched at $3/mo, then went to $3.99 and later $4/mo. Pro was $10+/mo (as of 2024). — [Starter Story](https://starterstory.com/stories/liinks-supercharge-your-link-in-bio); [Indie Hustle](https://indiehustle.beehiiv.com/p/simple-linkinbio-tool-making-20000-mrr)

**Features and templates**
- Blocks for links, images, videos, music embeds, contact forms, text, folders and social icons. Also themes and fonts, analytics (views, clicks, traffic sources), import from Linktree, Beacons and others, and an Instagram setup that pulls in the profile photo, bio and recent posts. The homepage does not give a template count. — [liinks.co](https://liinks.co)
- Signature feature: links in Instagram captions are added to the Liinks page automatically. Superframeworks says it had not seen this from other tools. — [Superframeworks](https://superframeworks.com/blog/liinks); [Indie Hustle](https://indiehustle.beehiiv.com/p/simple-linkinbio-tool-making-20000-mrr)
- Liinks runs SEO comparison pages such as liinks.co/compare/later. — [Liinks compare page](https://www.liinks.co/compare/later)

### Inferences
- The "10,000+ users, ~50% paid" claim is probably derived, not stated: 5,300 paying customers (2024) divided by "10,000+" (homepage, undated) is about 50%. Because there is no free plan, nearly every live account is either paying or on a trial. "Paid share" is therefore a misleading metric for Liinks. Trial-to-paid conversion is the metric that matters.
- At about $4-5 ARPU with roughly 5,300 payers, revenue works out to about $21-26K MRR, which agrees with the reported $25K.

### Gaps
- No primary source states a 50% paid share. The 10,000+ figure is undated marketing copy.
- No figures were found for 2025 or 2026 MRR, so it is unknown whether the $30K MRR goal for Sep 2025 was reached.
- The template count is not published.
- The Indie Hackers post (403) and the Product Hunt page could not be fetched directly.

## 2. How Liinks grew: outreach, custom pages, Product Hunt, ads, interviews

### Takeaway
Clark's own account (Starter Story, Oct 2024) confirms a hands-on outreach playbook. He contacted users of competing tools and offered to recreate their pages in Liinks, and he built pages for people who had none and invited them to try it. Other drivers were a #8 Product Hunt finish, Instagram ads (later Google Ads, after iOS 14), a "Made with Liinks" badge, price undercutting, and removing the free plan.

### Cited Findings
- Cold outreach: Clark contacted users of competing tools and offered to recreate their pages in Liinks. He also built pages for people not using any tool and invited them to try it. — [Starter Story, 2024-10-22](https://starterstory.com/stories/liinks-supercharge-your-link-in-bio)
- Product Hunt: about 300 upvotes and a #8 homepage rank. Some commenters dismissed it as "yet another link-in-bio tool." — [Starter Story](https://starterstory.com/stories/liinks-supercharge-your-link-in-bio)
- Paid ads: Instagram ads worked well at first but became less cost-effective after iOS 14, so he moved to Google Ads. — [Starter Story](https://starterstory.com/stories/liinks-supercharge-your-link-in-bio). Paid Instagram marketing also drove the first $1K MRR. — [Indie Hustle](https://indiehustle.beehiiv.com/p/simple-linkinbio-tool-making-20000-mrr)
- Viral loop: a "Made with Liinks" badge on pages sends traffic back from social profiles. — [Starter Story](https://starterstory.com/stories/liinks-supercharge-your-link-in-bio)
- Pricing strategy: he undercut Linktree, framed by Indie Hackers as "carved a $25k/mo niche out of a saturated market by undercutting the competition." — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/rRxyYFmG95zcSKSYdtM8)
- Moat argument: low price, design and customization, the Instagram caption auto-link feature, and close customer relationships. SaaSworthy reviews (29) praise design, "connection and care," and affordability. Sources cited include Clark's X thread (twitter.com/clarkcharlie03/status/1675516367825514497). — [Superframeworks](https://superframeworks.com/blog/liinks)
- Podcast and press appearances are listed on the Liinks press page, including a Growth Today episode. — [Liinks press](https://blog.liinks.co/press); [Growth Today #131](https://growthtoday.transistor.fm/131)
- Content SEO: Liinks runs a blog on link-in-bio strategy and competitor comparison pages. — [Liinks blog](https://liinks.co/blog/about); [compare/later](https://www.liinks.co/compare/later)
- Support as a growth lever: Trustpilot shows Liinks replying to 100% of negative reviews, usually within 24 hours. — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/liinks.co)

### Inferences
- The playbook to copy: (1) build the page for the creator before asking them to sign up, (2) target creators already paying a competitor and offer a free migration (Liinks now also has an import tool), (3) keep pricing below Linktree's, (4) remove the free plan once there is demand, to cut spam and force a trial decision, (5) rely on a badge loop and SEO comparison pages.
- Growth slowed to 11% a year once ad spend fell. The paid channel, not just organic, seems to have carried much of the growth.

### Gaps
- Starter Story does not say whether outreach went by DM or email, and gives no conversion rates for it.
- No exact date was found for the Product Hunt launch (Indie Hustle implies January 2021).

## 3. Creator segments and countries using Liinks and similar apps

### Takeaway
Liinks's evidence points to small businesses, service professionals and Instagram-first creators rather than models or adult creators. Testimonials and reviews include musicians, artists, estheticians, real estate agents, bridal hair and makeup artists, travel bloggers, speakers and authors, and agencies managing multiple profiles. Reviewers are mostly in the US, with some in Australia, Canada, Germany and Greece. Celebrity and music talent cluster on Hoo.be and Komi. Milkshake targeted mainly women building personal brands, starting in Australia.

### Cited Findings
- The Liinks homepage has 10 testimonials, attributed by role only: VP of Impact at Out in Tech, CEO of an animation studio, a travel blog, a real estate agent, an urban farmer, an artist, a speaker and author, a bridal hair and makeup artist, a communication and filmmaking professional, and a gaming-peripherals creator. No named example pages or showcase gallery is shown. — [liinks.co](https://liinks.co)
- Trustpilot has 82 reviews with a 4.6 TrustScore (14 in the last 12 months, the latest dated 2026-10-20). Reviewer niches include musicians and artists, small businesses, marketers managing brand pages, estheticians and service businesses, and people using it as a digital business card or QR code target. Visible reviewer countries are the US (majority), Australia, Canada, Germany and Greece. — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/liinks.co)
- Most Liinks users come from Instagram. — [Superframeworks](https://superframeworks.com/blog/liinks)
- The Pro tier targets agencies and businesses managing multiple brands. 500 of about 5,300 payers were on Pro (Oct 2024). — [Starter Story](https://starterstory.com/stories/liinks-supercharge-your-link-in-bio)
- Hoo.be users included Tom Brady, Jeff Bezos, Chris Hemsworth, Diplo, the Boston Celtics, Forever 21 and the Premier Lacrosse League, with access by invite only for people with thousands of followers (2022). — [Boston Globe, 2022-10-21](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2022/10/21/business/how-boston-startup-became-social-media-choice-celebrities-like-brady-bezos)
- Komi serves influencers, musicians, athletes and celebrities, including Lizzo, Usher, Meghan Trainor and Jessica Alba, and claims a combined reach of more than 2 billion followers across six continents (2023). — [TechCrunch, 2023-07-18](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2570230)
- Milkshake targeted mainly women building personal brands on Instagram and started with Australian users. Its 5-week early access drew more than 24,000 downloads across 95 countries. — [Campaign Brief](https://campaignbrief.com/envato-founders-launch-free-milkshake-app-for-instagrammers-to-make-instant-websites/); [SmartCompany](https://www.smartcompany.com.au/startupsmart/envato-cyan-taeed-milkshake/)

### Inferences
- Liinks's paying base looks like local and service small businesses plus Instagram creators in English-speaking markets. Its $5 price fits "solopreneur as creator" better than top influencers, who go to free, sales-led tools such as Hoo.be and Komi.
- Fashion, beauty, models and adult creators are not visible in Liinks's public evidence. That may be an under-served gap, or a segment Liinks avoids, given that it removed the free plan over spam and phishing.

### Gaps
- No public showcase of Liinks customer pages was found, and no country breakdown was published. Country data comes only from a sample of Trustpilot reviews.
- No evidence was found on use by OnlyFans or adult creators, or on adoption in LATAM or India, for any of these apps.

## 4. Quick profiles of competitors: Hoo.be, Komi, Milkshake, Bio.link, Snipfeed, Carrd, Later Linkin.bio

### Takeaway
The venture-backed design-first players (Hoo.be, Komi, Snipfeed) grew top-down through celebrity relationships and monetization features. Milkshake grew through a free mobile app aimed at Instagram women. Carrd and Liinks grew bootstrapped on cheap pricing. Several of these companies have since pivoted or been acquired, so the field is consolidating.

### Cited Findings
**Hoo.be (Boston, later moving to LA)**
- Founder and CEO Jordan Greenfield. Launched January 2021. Raised $3.5M in spring 2021, led by Ideanomics, with David Dobrik, Harry Jowsey and Big Night Entertainment Group participating. About 10 staff (2022). — [Boston Globe, 2022-10-21](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2022/10/21/business/how-boston-startup-became-social-media-choice-celebrities-like-brady-bezos)
- Caplight lists only about $583K raised (Series A, April 2021), which conflicts with the $3.5M figure. — [Caplight](https://www.caplight.com/company/hoobe-1)
- Model: free for creators. Revenue comes from larger clients (for example the Premier Lacrosse League) and from transaction fees on paywalled content. Access is invite-only. — [Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2022/10/21/business/how-boston-startup-became-social-media-choice-celebrities-like-brady-bezos)
- Growth tactics: nightlife and celebrity networking (Boston's Big Night clubs), doing favors for free (introducing 50 Cent to Robert Kraft), events such as "Hoo.be Fest" at David Dobrik's home, and a COVID-era tailwind. — [Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2022/10/21/business/how-boston-startup-became-social-media-choice-celebrities-like-brady-bezos)
- In April 2025 it partnered with Manifest Financial on creator business banking. — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/hoobie)

**Komi**
- Co-founder and CEO Lewis Crosbie. Launched in 2021 as a landing-page tool and opened to the public on 2023-07-18. Raised a $5M seed (Dec 2022, led by Contour) and a $12M Series A (led by RTP, with Sony Music and Live Nation among investors), for $17M in total. Priced at $9.99/mo or $96/yr with a 30-day trial. Features include multi-store cart checkout and email or phone capture. — [TechCrunch, 2023-07-18](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2570230)
- Caplight lists a $16M round dated 2024-07-29 and $24.07M in total, which is unverified and conflicts with other sources. — [Caplight](https://www.caplight.com/company/komi-2)
- In Nov 2025 it launched an AI-powered platform that connects creators with brands for brand deals. — [Net Influencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/komi-launches-ai-powered-platform-connecting-creators-with-brand-partnerships/); [Business Wire, 2025-11-18](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251118896938/en)

**Milkshake (Australia)**
- Founded by Cyan and Collis Ta'eed, the Envato co-founders. It is a free, mobile-only builder using "cards," with a median build time under 10 minutes in early access. Early access brought more than 24,000 downloads in 95 countries. — [Campaign Brief](https://campaignbrief.com/envato-founders-launch-free-milkshake-app-for-instagrammers-to-make-instant-websites/); [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/how-to/milkshake-website-builder-how-to-use-this-new-service)
- An undated third-party app listing shows 2M+ downloads and a 4.9 rating from 31K+ ratings. — [mwm.ai](https://mwm.ai/apps/id/1452719910)
- There is no desktop or web editor. — [Taplink review](https://taplink.at/en/blog/milkshake-app.html)

**Bio.link**
- A free-first tool known for a clean default look. Pro costs about $2/mo (removes branding) and Business about $7/mo (custom domain). It is reportedly owned by GoDaddy since 2022. That ownership claim comes from a competitor's blog (UniLink) and is unverified. — [UniLink review](https://www.unilink.us/blog/what-is-bio-link-tool)

**Snipfeed (LA/Paris)**
- Raised a $5.5M seed in Aug 2021 (CRV, Abstract Ventures, Michael Ovitz and others). Had 50,000 registered users, up 500% in 6 months (2021). Monetization features include paid content, personalized videos, livestream tickets, tips and paid 1:1 calls. — [TechCrunch, 2021-08-11](https://techcrunch.com/2021/08/11/link-in-bio-monetization-platform-snipfeed-raises-a-5-5m-seed-round/amp/); [Snipfeed blog](https://snipfeed.co/blog/snipfeed-raises-5-5m)
- Reportedly acquired by Planoly in April 2024. CB Insights lists $10.59M raised in total. — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/snipfeed)

**Carrd (comparison benchmark)**
- Solo founder AJ. About $30K MRR in 2020, then more than $1M ARR and more than 4M sites hosted, with $2M raised. Later about $2M ARR (2023) with a team of 4 and "zero marketing." Pricing is freemium with a Pro plan from $19/yr. — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/dbeoP4bDlEL3zzsivdgj); [SaaS Club](https://saasclub.io/?p=6013581)

**Later Linkin.bio**
- Bundled with Later's scheduling plans, with no standalone price. Third-party sources say plans start at about $18.75-25/mo. — [itechguides](https://www.itechguides.com/best/link-in-bio/later-linkin-bio/); [UniLink](https://app.unilink.us/blog/how-much-does-linktree-cost)

### Inferences
- There are two proven playbooks. The first is cheap, bootstrapped and product-led, with paid acquisition and personal outreach (Liinks, Carrd). The second is free and celebrity-led, monetizing through brands and transaction fees (Hoo.be, Komi), which needs capital and connections.
- The venture-backed players have drifted toward commerce and brand deals (Komi), banking partnerships (Hoo.be) or acquisition (Snipfeed). That leaves the "beautiful page for small creators" niche to cheap indie tools.

### Gaps
- Current (2025-2026) user and revenue figures were not found for Hoo.be, Komi, Milkshake or Bio.link.
- Current Hoo.be pricing and whether it is still invite-only were not checked.
- Bio.link's maker and ownership could not be confirmed (the site returned 403). The "Hey Taco" link mentioned in the brief could not be confirmed.
- Komi's headquarters country was not confirmed.
- G2 and Capterra pages for Liinks were not checked.
