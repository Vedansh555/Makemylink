# Underserved Creator Niches and Geographies in Link-in-Bio / Creator Storefronts (as of Oct 2026)

Research note: about 17 tool calls (searches plus fetches). Many figures come from third-party estimates (Sacra, vendor blogs) or older reports, and dates are flagged inline. Vendor blogs (UniLink, Taplink, UNUM, Foundry) sell competing tools, so their claims are marketing.

## Market baseline: size, concentration and who uses link-in-bio

### Takeaway
Linktree dominates by count (about 80% of Instagram link-in-bio users in a Nov 2024 sample; it claims 70M+ users), but the base is mostly small accounts (77% under 5k followers), skews female (64%) and is half US. Monetization is shifting to storefront players (Stan about $35M ARR, Whop about $142M annualized). Linktree's commerce features are US-only, which leaves non-US monetization structurally open.

### Cited Findings
- Analysis of 100M Instagram profiles found 31M link-in-bio users across 62 tools. Shares: Linktree 79.95% (24.7M), Milkshake 3.7%, Beacons 2.4%, Linkin.bio 1.6%, Bio.site 1.3%, Lnk.bio 0.9%, Instabio 0.9%, DistroKid 0.9%, Linkfire 0.7%, Carrd 0.7% (dated Nov 19, 2024) — [influencers.club](https://influencers.club/blog/state-of-the-link-in-bio-market/)
- Same dataset: 49% of users are in the US (15% of those in California). 53% are creators and 46.4% businesses. 64% are women. 40.4% have under 1k followers and 36.6% have 1k–5k. 55% of creators with 50k+ followers have done a brand collab. Top hashtags are #podcast, #vegan, #fashion, #travel (Nov 2024) — [influencers.club](https://influencers.club/blog/state-of-the-link-in-bio-market/)
- Linktree claims 70M+ users. Revenue estimates conflict: about $37.4M (2023, Sacra), $49.09M (2023, another Sacra page), and $61.6M with a $1.3B valuation (undated figure in a 2026 review) — [WebSearch summary of Wikipedia / Sacra / builtbyfoundry](https://sacra.com/c/linktree/valuation/); [Foundry review](https://www.builtbyfoundry.io/blog/linktree-review-2026-is-it-worth-it)
- Sacra says Linktree's commerce features (Shops, Sponsored Links, Rewards) are limited to US users, which caps transaction revenue — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/chat/h/aab1e515-45a7-413c-82c7-dbf6ff815f61/)
- In Dec 2022, Linktree had about 31M users and about $33M ARR, growing 29% YoY. Link-in-bio plans cost about $5–24/mo, against $29–299/mo for vertical SaaS like Shopify. The category had 40+ companies, with Beacons ($29M raised), Koji ($36M), Snipfeed ($6.4M) and Linkfire among them (OLD, 2022) — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/linktree-link-in-bio-onlyfans-dmz/)
- Stan: about $35M ARR in 2025, up 24% YoY (down from 93% growth in 2024). Single $29/mo tier, 0% take rate, gross churn roughly equal to new signups, 80k+ active creators. Its core audience is mid-tier creators in spirituality coaching, fitness, social media education and digital products (Sacra estimate) — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/stan); [Everything PR](https://everything-pr.com/stan-store-and-beacons-the-link-in-bio-platforms-that-became-creator-storefronts)
- Whop reportedly reached $142M annualized revenue by Oct 2025, up 255% YoY, which is more than Stan — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/stans-shopify-vs-whops-amazon/)
- Beacons (Aug 2026 review) has four tiers: $0, $10, $30 and $90/mo. Free and $10 tiers carry a 9% seller fee; the higher tiers carry 0%. Its media kit refreshes live from follower count, engagement and demographics. Sources conflict on tier prices (PricingSaaS lists $75 for Max, probably the annual rate) — [Foundry](https://www.builtbyfoundry.io/blog/beacons-review-2026-is-the-9-percent-fee-worth-it); [PricingSaaS](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/beacons)

### Inferences
- The free, generic link-list layer is saturated and owned by Linktree. A new entrant can win where Linktree's US-only commerce and generic templates fail: non-US payments, vertical-specific workflows, or verticals with policy restrictions.
- Since 77% of users have under 5k followers, the bulk of the market has low willingness to pay. Revenue concentrates in the mid-tier "middle-class creator" (Stan's $29/mo base), so aim outreach at the 10k–500k band.
- Stan's churn problem suggests that flat-fee storefront SaaS for coaches is crowded and churny. Differentiation should come through vertical depth, not price alone.

### Gaps
- No 2025/2026 Linktree-published creator report was found, and no niche-level (vertical) breakdown of link-in-bio users.
- No country-level breakdown outside the US share (49%).

## Fashion, beauty and lifestyle creators

### Takeaway
This is the largest and best-monetized vertical, and also the most contested. LTK (about 300k creators, $6B+ in annual retail sales driven) and ShopMy (about 175k creators, 50k+ brands) own affiliate storefronts. The gap is a unified, beautiful page that combines LTK, ShopMy and Amazon links, shoppable looks and a live media kit, especially for micro creators and non-US creators that LTK under-serves.

### Cited Findings
- LTK creators drive more than $6B in annual retail sales (LTK 2026 Awards release, Sep 1, 2026) — [BusinessWire](https://secure.businesswire.com/news/home/20260901107046/en/LTK-Names-the-Creators-and-Brands-Shaping-Culture-and-Commerce-in-the-2026-LTK-Awards)
- LTK reaches about 40M monthly shoppers and 300k+ creators globally (third-party reference, 2026, not official) — [FemFounded](https://femfounded.org/reference/ltk)
- LTK had nearly 200k creators and $3B+ annual retail sales in 2023, with about 20M app MAU (undated) and 5,000–6,000 brands. ShopMy had about 175k creators (late 2025) and 50k+ commissionable brands. Commissions run 10–30% (ShopMy) and 10–25% (LTK), against 1–5% for the Amazon Influencer Program. ShopMy has no follower minimum; LTK historically vetted creators and has widened micro-influencer access by 2026 — [Stack Influence](https://stackinfluence.com/blog/shopmy-vs-ltk-best-affiliate-platform-2026)
- LTK saw a 70% spike in UK creator applications (Dec 2023, OLD) — [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20231219946975/en/Everybody-Wants-to-Be-a-Creator-Creator-Commerce-Platform-LTK-Sees-70-Spike-in-Applications-in-the-UK)
- #fashion is among the top four hashtags of link-in-bio users, and 64% of link-in-bio users are women (Nov 2024) — [influencers.club](https://influencers.club/blog/state-of-the-link-in-bio-market/)
- Beacons offers a live-updating media kit with rate card, used for sponsorship negotiation — [Foundry](https://www.builtbyfoundry.io/blog/beacons-review-2026-is-the-9-percent-fee-worth-it)

### Inferences
- Fashion creators commonly juggle LTK, ShopMy, Amazon Storefront and brand codes. LTK and ShopMy pages are platform-branded and siloed, so a neutral "shop my looks" hub with an image grid, outfit hotspots, discount-code wallet and media kit is a plausible wedge. This is a feature gap inferred from product positioning, not verified by a survey.
- Willingness to pay is likely higher than average because creators earn affiliate income. But the tools that pay them (LTK, ShopMy) are free, so the entrant must charge for design, analytics and brand-deal tooling, not for commerce itself.
- Outreach is easy: creators are highly visible on Instagram and TikTok, DMs are common, and custom pages built from their existing LTK/ShopMy links make an instant demo.
- Competition is high. "Become #1" is unrealistic globally, but realistic in a sub-segment (for example UK/EU or India fashion micro creators, or modest-fashion creators in the Middle East).

### Gaps
- No verified total count of fashion/beauty creators, and no data on what share pay for link-in-bio tools.
- No data found on Liinks' user base or pricing.
- No survey quantifying multi-platform affiliate juggling.

## Non-US / international creators

### Takeaway
International markets are the clearest structural opportunity. Linktree's monetization is US-only, local payment rails (UPI, Pix, M-Pesa, QRIS) are poorly served by US tools, and strong regional players exist but are fragmented and focused on courses or consultations, not polished storefront/link pages. India (2–2.5M monetized creators, only 8–10% monetizing effectively) and Brazil (a mature infoproduct economy via Hotmart/Kiwify) are the biggest. Price sensitivity is high.

### Cited Findings
**India**
- There are 2–2.5M monetized creators, influencing $350–400B in consumer spend, projected above $1T by 2030. Only 8–10% monetize effectively, against 40%+ in mature markets. Most nano and micro creators earn under INR 18,000/mo (about $200). Direct creator ecosystem revenue is $20–25B, projected to reach $100–125B. In Tier 2/3 towns, over 60% of consumers are influenced by creators (BCG, May 2025) — [BCG](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2025/india-from-content-to-commerce-mapping-indias-creator-economy); [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/amp/how-it-works/indias-creator-economy-influences-400-bn-in-consumer-spend-set-to-drive-1-tn-by-2030-bcg-64390.htm)
- Cosmofeed launched SuperProfile in Feb 2023 as a link-in-bio for India's nano and micro creators, positioned against Linktree with a fintech angle. It raised a $1.5M seed in Mar 2022 (OLD) — [BusinessWorld](https://businessworld.in/article/superprofile-by-cosmofeed-a-tool-for-content-creators-to-effectively-monetise-their-content-464911); [ITVoice](https://www.itvoice.in/cosmofeed-takes-on-linktree-in-india-by-launching-unique-fintech-offering-for-creators-to-monetise-content-via-superprofile); [Entrepreneur India](https://india.entrepreneur.com/finance/cosmofeed-raises-15-million-in-seed-round/422369)
- Topmate (founded 2021) is listed as bootstrapped by Inc42, with about 2.02M monthly web visits. CB Insights shows an $850K raise in May 2022. Its model is paid expert and creator consultations — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/company/topmate-io/); [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/topmateio/financials)

**Brazil / LATAM**
- Kiwify has 30k+ active infoproduct creators and 25M+ registered buyers, grew 62.42% in 2024, and raised R$150M through a FIDC in Feb 2025 after R$50M in late 2024 — [Finsiders](https://finsidersbrasil.com.br/conteudo-de-marca/kiwify-conclui-nova-rodada-de-captacao-de-r-150-milhoes-com-seu-fidc)
- In an FGV/Hotmart survey (612 creators, Aug–Sep 2024), 42% said digital product sales were their main income, and formalized producers earn about 2x more — [FGV](https://portal.fgv.br/en/noticia/digital-content-creation-grows-30-and-boosts-brazilian-job-market-survey-shows)
- Hotmart reports US$10B cumulative partner sales (Mar 2024). AI products generated R$140M+ on Hotmart in 2025 from 340k+ buyers — [Mercado&Consumo](https://mercadoeconsumo.com.br/21/03/2024/inovacao/hotmart-plataforma-de-venda-online-alcanca-us-10-bilhoes-em-vendas-de-parceiros/); [Startups.com.br](https://startups.com.br/negocios/inteligencia-artificial/hotmart-aposta-em-ia-para-ampliar-receita-ate-em-produtos-fisicos/)
- Taplink (strong in CIS) actively targets Brazil with Portuguese "link na bio" content. Its Pro plan is listed around R$18/mo (older guide), and it claims 50–60+ payment gateways. No Pix confirmation was found — [Taplink PT-BR](https://taplink.at/pt-br/blog/como-criar-um-linktree.html); [Taplink review](https://taplink.at/en/blog/what-is-taplink.html)

**Africa**
- Selar (Nigeria) supports NGN, USD, GHS, KES, ZAR and GBP, plus Paystack, Stripe, PayPal and M-Pesa. It expanded to Kenya in Aug 2024. Its creators average about $100/mo, and it processed over Sh520M in payments to African creators in a year (Jun 2025) — [TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2024/01/24/selar-africa-creator-economy/); [Capital FM](https://www.capitalfm.co.ke/news/2025/06/tackling-monetization-barriers-in-kenyas-creator-economy)

**Southeast Asia**
- Lynk.id is an Indonesian link-in-bio with digital products, subscriptions and direct payments, popular with micro-businesses. Its founding date is inconsistent across sources. Oneblink (MTARGET) is a local competitor. Linktree has publicly planned Indonesia support (undated) — [Telkom University](https://it.telkomuniversity.ac.id/en/what-is-lynk-id/); [DailySocial](https://en.dailysocial.id/post/Linktree-plans-to-support-creators-and-businesses-in-Indonesia)

**Middle East**
- Dubai's Creators HQ aims to attract 10,000 influencers with 10-year Golden Visas and a $40.8M Content Creators Support Fund (Feb 2025). Eligibility details conflict across law-firm sources. The UAE also requires influencer licensing (NMC) — [IMI Daily](https://www.imidaily.com/mena/dubai-launches-golden-visa-program-for-content-creators/); [The Week](https://www.theweek.in/news/middle-east/2025/02/02/dubai-offers-golden-visa-with-10-year-residency-for-content-creators-influencers.amp.html); [Neolegal](https://neolegal.ae/insights/nmc-mro-influencer-licence-uae)

### Inferences
- **India** has the largest headcount but the lowest ARPU (creators earn about $200/mo). Pricing must be roughly INR 99–499/mo, or take-rate based with UPI checkout. Incumbents (Topmate for consultations, SuperProfile and Cosmofeed for paywalled content, Graphy for courses) are thinly funded (seed-stage, 2022), and none is clearly the #1 aesthetic link page for India's fashion/beauty and regional-language creators. Outreach is very feasible: Instagram DMs and WhatsApp are norms.
- **Brazil**: payments are heavily solved by Hotmart and Kiwify for infoproducts, so a link page with native Pix plus Hotmart/Kiwify affiliate-link integration and pt-BR design could be a #1 candidate in "link na bio". The main competition is Taplink and Linktree.
- **Middle East (UAE/KSA)**: there are fewer creators but higher ARPU, plus Arabic/RTL and licensing-display needs (showing the NMC licence number on the page). No dedicated Arabic-first link-in-bio player was found, which is a potential niche to become #1 in but needs validation.
- **Africa/SEA**: these have low ARPU and existing local players (Selar, Lynk.id), and are lower priority for revenue.

### Gaps
- Pix support in Taplink, Linktree or Beacons is unverified. UPI support in Linktree is unverified. QRIS support in Lynk.id is unverified.
- No 2026 funding or revenue data for Topmate, SuperProfile or Graphy.
- No data on Arabic-language link-in-bio tools or GCC creator counts.
- No UK/EU-specific link-in-bio data beyond LTK's 2023 UK application spike.

## Other verticals: musicians, adult creators, coaches, real estate, local businesses

### Takeaway
Adult creators are the most clearly underserved by policy: Linktree restricts them and adult-friendly tools (AllMyLinks and others) are a quality-light niche. They also have high willingness to pay and a big ecosystem (OnlyFans had $932M revenue and $4.8B GMV in the 2022 data). Musicians are served by smart-link tools (Linkfire, Feature.fm, ToneDen, DistroKid). Real-estate agents and local businesses have high ARPU and clear lead-gen needs, but are served by generic tools and native Instagram multi-links.

### Cited Findings
- Linktree banned accounts linking to sexual services, and linking out to such material via the bio link was not allowed (older Motherboard report; check current terms) — [nordot/Motherboard](https://nordot.app/855765278624464896)
- Sacra framed link-in-bio as the "SFW DMZ" in front of OnlyFans. OnlyFans had $932M revenue and $4.8B GMV (Dec 2022, OLD) — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/linktree-link-in-bio-onlyfans-dmz/)
- AllMyLinks explicitly allows adult creators. Beacons reportedly allows adult content. Linktree allows it only within content guidelines (vendor blog, 2026) — [UniLink](https://app.unilink.us/blog/linktree-vs-allmylinks)
- Music: Linkfire costs about $9.99/mo with no free tier, Feature.fm about $5/mo, and ToneDen about $50/mo (single comparison source; verify). Linkfire suits campaigns and pre-saves; ToneDen suits ad tooling — [Orphiq](https://orphiq.com/resources/link-in-bio-tools-musicians); [link.boo](https://link.boo/guides/best-music-smart-link-tools)
- DistroKid (0.9%) and Linkfire (0.7%) appear in the top 10 link-in-bio tools, and #podcast is the #1 hashtag among link-in-bio users (Nov 2024) — [influencers.club](https://influencers.club/blog/state-of-the-link-in-bio-market/)
- Real estate: guides say an agent's link should show listings, book showings and consults, show reviews, and split buyer, seller and renter paths. A generic brokerage homepage makes a poor bio link. Instagram's native multi-link is an option with less customization (2026, vendor-heavy sources) — [UNUM](https://www.unum.la/blog/best-link-in-bio-real-estate-agents); [UniLink](https://www.unilink.us/blog/link-in-bio-for-real-estate-agents-2026); [Coffee & Contracts](https://coffeecontracts.com/blog/10-instagram-features-you-may-not-know-about-for-real-estate-agents)
- Fitness and coaching is Stan's core segment ($29/mo), alongside spirituality and social-media education — [Everything PR](https://everything-pr.com/stan-store-and-beacons-the-link-in-bio-platforms-that-became-creator-storefronts)
- Link-in-bio users are 46.4% businesses (Nov 2024) — [influencers.club](https://influencers.club/blog/state-of-the-link-in-bio-market/)

### Inferences
- **Adult creators**: they have high willingness to pay (they monetize directly), need SFW-compliant landing pages, age gates, multi-platform links (OnlyFans, Fansly) and anti-ban link cloaking, and are reachable via X, Reddit and agencies. Risks include payment processor (Stripe) restrictions and brand reputation. Competition is fragmented and low-quality.
- **Musicians/DJs**: needs are largely met by smart-link tools. A wedge exists for DJs (booking EPK, mixes, gig calendar, press kit) rather than release marketing, but this is unverified.
- **Real estate**: highest ARPU potential (agents pay $50–300/mo for CRM and IDX tools elsewhere; unverified here), but you must compete with brokerage-provided sites. Outreach is easy via Instagram and public listings.
- **Models/photographers/tattoo artists**: portfolio-first, booking and deposit needs. No data found; likely served by Carrd (0.7%), Squarespace and booking apps. Tattoo artists need deposit collection and flash galleries, a plausible but unvalidated niche.
- **Restaurants/local businesses**: served by Linktree, Linkin.bio (Later) and Google Business. Low WTP for link-in-bio specifically.

### Gaps
- No size data for tattoo artists, models, DJs or restaurants using link-in-bio.
- No verified current Linktree adult policy text; Beacons' adult policy was not verified on its own site.
- No real-estate WTP data from a primary source.

## Candidate ranking (synthesis for report writer)

### Takeaway
The best candidates for becoming #1 or building meaningful revenue are, in order:
1. India fashion/beauty/lifestyle micro creators, with UPI and affiliate support.
2. Fashion/beauty affiliate-hub creators in the US/UK (an aggregator over LTK, ShopMy and Amazon, plus a media kit).
3. Adult creators (SFW-safe hubs).
4. Brazil "link na bio" with Pix and Hotmart/Kiwify.
5. GCC Arabic/RTL creators.
6. Real-estate agents.
7. DJs and tattoo artists (booking and deposits).
8. Coaches/fitness (crowded: Stan, Whop).

### Cited Findings
- Size and ARPU anchors come from the sections above: India 2–2.5M monetized creators at under $200/mo — [BCG](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2025/india-from-content-to-commerce-mapping-indias-creator-economy); LTK 300k creators and $6B GMV — [BusinessWire](https://secure.businesswire.com/news/home/20260901107046/en/LTK-Names-the-Creators-and-Brands-Shaping-Culture-and-Commerce-in-the-2026-LTK-Awards); Stan $29/mo and churn — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/stan); Linktree commerce US-only — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/chat/h/aab1e515-45a7-413c-82c7-dbf6ff815f61/)

### Inferences
| # | Niche | Audience size (evidence) | WTP / ARPU | Competition | Outreach ease |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | India fashion/beauty/lifestyle micro creators (UPI, Amazon/Myntra affiliate, regional language) | 2–2.5M monetized creators (BCG 2025) | Low ($1–6/mo or take rate) | Medium; seed-stage locals, Linktree without local commerce | Very high (IG DMs, WhatsApp) |
| 2 | US/UK fashion and beauty affiliate creators (LTK + ShopMy + Amazon hub, shoppable looks, live media kit) | 300k LTK + 175k ShopMy creators | Medium-high ($10–30/mo) | High (Linktree, Beacons, Liinks, LTK's own pages) | High |
| 3 | Adult creators (SFW hubs, age gate, cloaking) | Large; OnlyFans $4.8B GMV (2022) | High | Low-medium, fragmented (AllMyLinks etc.) | High via X and Reddit, but payment-processor risk |
| 4 | Brazil "link na bio" (Pix, Hotmart/Kiwify integration) | 30k+ Kiwify creators; large Hotmart base | Low-medium (R$18–50/mo) | Medium (Taplink, Linktree) | High (IG/WhatsApp) |
| 5 | GCC Arabic/RTL creators (licence display, bilingual pages) | Small; Dubai targets 10k creators | High | Low (no Arabic-first player found) | Medium |
| 6 | Real-estate agents (listings, showing booking, lead capture) | Large (count not sourced) | High | Medium (generic tools, brokerage sites) | High (public listings) |
| 7 | DJs, tattoo artists, models (booking, deposits, EPK/portfolio) | Unknown | Medium | Low-medium | High |
| 8 | Coaches/fitness | Large | Medium ($29/mo norm) | Very high (Stan, Whop, Beacons) | High, but crowded |

### Gaps
- Audience sizes for #5–#7 are unsourced, and ARPU figures in the table are partly inference. Validate them with direct outreach tests.
